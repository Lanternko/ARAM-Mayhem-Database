Sett ARAM Mayhem analysis

Sett has a Bayesian-adjusted win rate of 53.4% in ARAM Mayhem (patch 26.23), ranking #16 of 173 champions over 13,881 games. That puts it among the strongest picks this patch.

This is a historical association that may reflect game state (for example items bought while behind), not proof the item itself is bad.

On team composition, Sett wins more on Mage core teams (+0.4 pts) and less on AD carry teams (−0.4 pts).
