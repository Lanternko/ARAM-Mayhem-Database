Kennen ARAM Mayhem analysis

Kennen has a Bayesian-adjusted win rate of 44.9% in ARAM Mayhem (patch 26.23), ranking #171 of 173 champions over 14,156 games. That is on the weak side this patch, so augment and build choices matter more.
